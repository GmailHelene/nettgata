# Pricing, unit economics, onboarding, retention, trust and channel models of website and webshop platforms (state as of October 2026)

Research date: 2026-10-31. Currency is stated per finding (USD unless noted). Source-quality warning that applies throughout: many "pricing guide" pages are run by competitors or affiliates (for example UENI, We.Inc, AgentSite, Nettify, Smartbyra, Figment, Flowrush, Webnewbiz). They are useful for list prices but are biased on comparisons. Primary sources (earnings releases, call transcripts, vendor help centres) are marked "primary" where I used them. Squarespace has been private since October 2024, so its post-2024 financial statistics are mostly unreliable or absent.

---

## 1. Pricing models: freemium, trials, tiers, transaction fees, AI credits, annual discounts, add-ons, "website as a service" (WaaS)

### Takeaway
The market has converged on: a free or 14-day entry, 3-4 tiers from roughly $12-19 to $99-159 per month on annual billing, 24-36% discount for annual commitment, AI metered by credits (monthly caps that do not roll over), and revenue from payments, domain, email and business add-ons on top of the subscription. WaaS (done-for-you with human help) sits at $15-199 per month plus $0-2,500 setup in the US, and roughly 399-1,999 NOK per month in Norway.

### Inferences
- Global DIY builders anchor entry price at about $12-29 per month (annual). A Norwegian platform charging below roughly 250 kr per month will compete on price with Wix Light/Squarespace/GoDaddy; 399-1,499 kr per month is where Norwegian "built for you" competitors already sit, and agencies sit at 1,000-3,000 kr per month after 15,000-40,000 kr setup. A done-for-you tier at 499-999 kr per month would undercut agencies strongly while sitting at or above DIY.
- Annual discount of 24-36% is standard across Wix, Squarespace, Duda, Shopify, Framer and Webflow. Offering a monthly option at 30-40% premium and annual as default is the market norm.
- Every vendor caps AI by credits that do not roll over (Squarespace 10-120, Hostinger 5-400, GoDaddy 50-750, Duda 150 per account, Webflow add-on $20, Lovable top-ups $0.30). This is consistent with real inference cost being material relative to a $10-25 plan (see section 2).
- Introductory-price-then-renewal-jump (Hostinger 3.7x, GoDaddy about 1.6-2x) is the single largest generator of trust complaints (section 5); a transparent flat renewal price is a differentiator.
- Transaction fees: platform fee on payments is a major revenue lever (Shopify merchant solutions, Wix transaction revenue). Norwegian differentiation: Vipps (and Klarna) built in at 0% platform fee.

### Gaps
- No reliable Norwegian NOK price for Wix and Squarespace on their own Norwegian pages (only a competitor blog). Need a direct check of wix.com/no and squarespace.com NOK prices.
- Hostinger and Durable add-on prices for domain/email in NOK not found.
- Durable's current Starter tier status unresolved.
- Webflow/Framer prices vary between sources ($14 vs $15 vs $18 for Webflow Basic); use Webflow's own page.

## 2. Unit economics: ARPU, gross margin, CAC, payback, churn, NRR; hosting and LLM costs per site

### Takeaway
Public numbers: Wix creative subscriptions gross margin 80% (down from 85% due to AI), Shopify subscription gross margin just under 80%, GoDaddy ARPU $250 with retention above 85%, Wix NRR 105% (FY2025), Wix time to return on acquisition marketing 7-9 months. Squarespace, Duda and Hostinger do not disclose CAC/churn/gross margin. SMB SaaS monthly churn benchmarks are 3-7% (secondary sources); platforms with hosting, domain and payments bundled show far lower effective churn. A complete AI generation of one site costs roughly $0.50-$3 in API spend at current frontier-mid model prices; hosting a static site costs near zero on Cloudflare.

### Inferences
- Implied monthly customer churn at GoDaddy: if 85% annual retention applies to customers, that is about 1.3% per month (1 minus 0.85 to the power of 1/12). That is far below SMB SaaS benchmarks of 3-7%, which fits a bundle of domains + hosting + email + site where cancelling means taking down the company address. GoDaddy's retention definition may be revenue- or customer-based; not specified in the sources.
- Wix ARPU is not disclosed; Squarespace ARPUS $225 per year (about $19 per month) and GoDaddy ARPU $250 per year (about $21 per month) bracket what a mass-market platform earns per customer. Shopify's subscription is only about 22% of revenue (802 / 3,583); payments and apps are the rest. Wix transaction revenue is a smaller part.
- A static or lightly dynamic customer site costs a few cents to a few dollars per month in infrastructure if built on a CDN/edge host with a shared multi-tenant app; the dominant variable costs are AI usage, support, payment fraud/chargebacks, email and domains.
- One-time AI generation at about $0.5-3 per site is trivial against a $20-50 per month plan (payback within the first month), but unlimited AI edits are not: if an edit/chat turn costs 3-10 US cents, 150 changes per month is $5-15, a large share of a $20-30 plan. This is why all vendors cap and meter AI (my estimate, not a sourced figure).
- Wix's 7-9 month "time to return" on acquisition marketing is a useful ceiling for a self-serve acquisition motion; a done-for-you offer with human onboarding needs CAC payback inside 12 months at 24-36% annual-plan discounts.
- Free tiers with AI are expensive: Wix reported free-user inference as a headwind; Durable limits free to 5 images and 10 AI messages; Squarespace has no free tier at all.

### Gaps
- No public CAC, payback, or churn figures for Squarespace, Duda, Hostinger, Shopify (beyond S&M less than 14% of revenue), or Wix's logo churn. Only Wix NRR (105%) and GoDaddy retention (above 85%) are company-stated.
- ProfitWell/Paddle, ChartMogul, KeyBanc/Sapphire primary reports were not opened directly; benchmark values come from secondary summaries and should be treated as indicative. Several summaries cite "OpenView 2026" which is doubtful because OpenView stopped publishing; do not cite that.
- No source gave real per-site infrastructure cost at Wix/Squarespace/Duda; Cloudflare/Vercel pricing is the only proxy.
- No measured LLM cost for "maintaining" a site over a month; only per-build and per-conversation numbers.

### Inferences
- Time-to-first-draft is no longer a differentiator (everyone is at seconds-to-minutes). The real gap is time to publish-ready: generic AI copy and stock images mean 1-4 hours of editing. - Human touch is cheap relative to LTV for service businesses: UENI-style 30-minute launch call plus 30 days of edits is the standard concierge pattern; Durable reserves its call for the $41 per month tier.

### Gaps
- No publish-rate or activation-rate data from Wix, Squarespace, Shopify or Durable (all internal). The only public "launch rate" is an unsourced marketing claim.
- No source documented a Google Business Profile import in Wix/Squarespace; only Durable (connect) and QuikWebsites (demo from listing) mention it.
- No independent data on whether concierge onboarding improves 12-month retention.

## 4. Retention levers: bundling domain/email, payments, bookings, CRM, recurring value, switching costs vs open export, annual billing, education/community

### Takeaway
The strongest evidenced levers are (1) annual or multi-year billing, (2) multi-product attach (GoDaddy: over half of customers have 2+ paid products, retention above 85%), (3) embedded payments (Shopify: payments are 68% of GMV, merchant solutions ~78% of revenue), and (4) being the home of the domain and email. Switching costs from closed formats clearly exist (Wix, Squarespace export almost nothing) but come with a measurable trust and review penalty. Direct data on community/education effects and on monthly report features is thin.

### Cited Findings
- Annual billing: Wix reports more users (especially Base44) choosing annual plans "as trust grows", with retention improving; Squarespace/Duda/Webflow/Framer all discount annual by about 24-36%; Hostinger sells 48-month prepay; monthly price premium on Wix about 30-40%. — [Wix Q1 2026 call](https://fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/13/wixcom-wix-q1-2026-earnings-call-transcript?referring_guid=ec99f021-e678-4aba-953a-5774720501ff); [Refact 2026-07-02](https://refact.co/insights/digital-product/wix-website-cost)
- Annual subscribers churn at roughly one-third the monthly rate and switching default to annual with a 15-20% discount cuts churn 40-60% (secondary SaaS benchmark claims, no primary study cited). — [Fairview 2026-05-29](https://getfairview.com/blog/saas-churn-rate-benchmarks); [StackedReview](https://stackedreview.com/saas-churn-rate-statistics)
- Multi-product: GoDaddy says over 50% of customers have at least two paid products and retention is above 85%; Airo users: over 70% have two or more products. — [MarketBeat transcript](https://marketbeat.com/earnings/reports/2026-7-30-godaddy-inc-stock)
- Wix Business Solutions (Google Workspace, email marketing, paid ads) is a growing recurring line (+14%, with Google Workspace and Paid Ads cited as growth drivers); Wix 2025 NRR 105%. — [Zacks/Yahoo 2026-08-04](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/wix-q2-earnings-beat-expectations-140700400.html); [Wix Q4 2025 call](https://fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/03/04/wixcom-wix-q4-2025-earnings-call-transcript?referring_guid=98bb6095-e31e-46ec-92d5-d9b603fdcb8c)
- Embedded payments: Shopify payments penetration 68% of GMV (+3 points y/y); Wix take rate 1.96% on $3.6B GPV in a quarter; Squarespace charges 2% store fee on Basic and processing 2.5-2.9% + $0.30; Duda and Hostinger advertise 0% platform fee. — [Fool Shopify transcript](https://fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/12/shopify-shop-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript?referring_guid=822e95bd-805d-447d-9b2b-519b920a2406); [Yahoo/Zacks Wix](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/wix-q2-earnings-beat-expectations-140700400.html)
- Recurring-value features: Durable's AI agents (blog posts, review requests, competitor research, lead replies, weekly AI visibility ranking), Squarespace Beacon AI/AIO Scanner/SEO Scanner (Core+), Wix Harmony agents (Kleo, Juno, Omni). — [Tested Intelligence](https://testedintelligence.com/durable-review-2026); [GoogieHost](https://googiehost.com/blog/squarespace-ai-website-builder-review); [Wix blog](https://wix.com/blog/wix-artificial-design-intelligence)
- Bolt's CEO: the AI app builder category churns 20-40% because projects are one-off, while Wix's recurring revenue is mostly hosting and services people keep paying for. — [Host0 2026-10-04](https://host0.ai/blog/state-of-vibe-coding-2026)
- Lock-in: Wix cannot export the site (only products, CMS collections, contacts, orders, domain); Squarespace exports a WordPress-format XML with some pages and one blog but no design; Shopify exports products/customers/orders as CSV and theme as Liquid; Webflow exports HTML/CSS/JS on paid workspace plans without CMS/forms/ecommerce; WordPress and WooCommerce export everything; GoDaddy Airo allows code download on paid plans; Durable has no HTML export; Hostinger's export moves content but not design. — [PandaCodeGen 2026-10-04](https://pandacodegen.com/blog/website-portability); [WebBuilding.net 2026-10-02](https://webbuilding.net/guides/leaving-your-website-builder); [WebHostTraining](https://webhosttraining.com/hostinger-ai-website-builder-review); [Noonwp Durable review 2026-06-30](https://noonwp.com/review/durable-review)
- Cost to leave Wix estimated $7,000-20,000 and Squarespace $5,000-15,000 for a typical small business (agency blog, not a study). — [Pryce Digital 2026-03-25](https://prycedigital.com/blog/hidden-cost-of-website-platform-lock-in)
- Wix's own blog now tells AI-site owners to "check that you can export your files", while its help centre says Wix sites must run on Wix servers. — [UltimateWB 2026-08-28](https://ultimatewb.com/blog/13207/wix-says-ai-website-owners-should-care-about-portability-theres-just-one-problem)
- Bundling evidence (non-website, consumer streaming): ESPN+ churn about 8% standalone vs 3% in bundle (Antenna); Disney+ six-month churn 43% standalone vs 19% in bundle (Ampere). — [Bango 2025-07-29](https://bango.com/customer-retention-reducing-churn-through-multi-party-bundles)
- Domain + email lock-in is framed as operational stickiness rather than contract (hosting vendor blog; vendor-interest). — [Hostopia 2026-06-01](https://blog.hostopia.com/bundled-digital-services-smb-churn)

- Failed payments: involuntary churn 20-40% of total churn; smart dunning recovers 50-80%. — [Fairview](https://getfairview.com/blog/saas-churn-rate-benchmarks)

### Inferences
- The best-evidenced bundle for a small-business site platform is domain + business email + hosting + payments (Vipps) + a recurring "value" feature (monthly visibility report, AI blog/FAQ suggestions, review requests). GoDaddy's 85% retention with 50%+ multi-product attach is the closest large-scale proof; there is no website-specific controlled study.
- Closed formats retain customers (Wix NRR 105%) but erode trust (section 5). Offering a documented full export (HTML or WordPress/portable stack plus content and order data) is a trust differentiator rather than a retention risk, because retention can rest on service, domain/email, payments and recurring value instead of format lock-in. This is my reasoning from the sources, not a measured result.
- Dunning, card-expiry handling and clear renewal reminders matter: 20-40% of churn is involuntary and billing surprise is the top complaint theme.

### Gaps
- No quantified A/B or cohort data for monthly reports, AI content suggestions, community or education on website-builder retention.
- Bundling statistics from "winsavvy" and similar blogs looked like SEO content with no traceable studies, so they were excluded.
- No data on how much Vipps/booking/CRM attach reduces churn in the Nordics.

## 5. Trust: challenger playbooks, guarantees, case studies, transparent pricing, data ownership, local support, certifications, ratings

### Takeaway
Trust for challengers was built through (a) a clear wedge and a platform ecosystem that others profit from (Shopify), (b) sustained brand investment (Squarespace, 12 consecutive Super Bowls), (c) visible human support and low-risk guarantees, and (d) in 2026, security certifications and portability. ### Inferences
- Complaint data point to the cheapest trust wins: no surprise renewals (show the renewal date and price in the dashboard and by email 30 days ahead), reachable humans in Norwegian, refunds without friction, and domain registered in the customer's name. These cost little and address 70%+ of one-star themes in the one study found (support, refunds, billing).
- "As safe as WordPress, Shopify or an agency" needs proof on four axes: portability (full export plus content/order data), identity (domain owner is the customer), operations (uptime, backups, EU data hosting, named Norwegian support person), and independent assurance (ISO 27001 or SOC 2 are now table stakes for AI builders selling to businesses; they are not typical for SMB website builders, so absence is not disqualifying but presence is a differentiator). Whether ISO 27001 is worth the cost for a small Norwegian vendor is not answered by sources.
- Local proof: Vipps integration, Brønnøysund/organisasjonsnummer-based profile import, .no domains and Norwegian case studies are plausible trust signals; sources are weak, so test with customers rather than assume.

### Gaps

- No Norwegian-specific Trustpilot ratings or reviews for platforms; no Norwegian study on trust in website builders vs agencies.
- Durable's and UENI's Trustpilot claims are vendor-reported; not independently verified here.
- Case-study effectiveness (for example conversion lift from named Norwegian case studies) not found.

## 6. Hybrid and channel models: expert marketplaces, partner revenue shares, white-label, agency share of new sites

### Takeaway
Partners are large and strategically contested. At Wix, "Partners" (agencies, freelancers and B2B resellers) are 38% of revenue ($213.8M in Q2 2026, +17%) but turned weak in May-June 2026 and triggered a guidance cut; analysts link this to AI building lowering demand for agencies. Platforms pay partners mostly through recurring revenue share (Shopify 20% of subscription plus 0.1% of GMV for four years from 2026-08-10; Wix 20-30% on Studio plans; Squarespace 20-40% lump sum on annual plans) or sell wholesale per-site pricing (Duda).

### Inferences
- Agencies/freelancers account for a large and sticky share of platform revenue (Wix 38% including resellers; Shopify's partner ecosystem created most of its early growth), so a freelancer/agency channel is a proven route to trust and distribution. Helene-type single-person freelancer use case fits "white-label + client billing" (Duda style) or "referral rev-share" (Shopify/Squarespace style).
- A new Norwegian platform can price a partner tier at a wholesale per-site fee of roughly $14-17 per site per month (Duda) with a branded dashboard and client billing, or a revenue share of 20-30% of annual list price; Shopify's four-year cap and Wix's restriction to Studio plans show that platforms tune partner pay down as the ecosystem matures.
- The Wix 2026 experience shows that AI that lets owners build themselves cannibalises the agency channel unless the platform provides a role for the expert (review, finishing, domain/email, SEO, care plans). A hybrid "AI builds, expert finishes" tier is consistent with Squarespace's own suggestion of a fixed-scope human finish (8-15 hours for a five-page site).

### Gaps
- No platform publishes the share of new sites built by agencies/freelancers (Wix only reports partner revenue share; Shopify no current figure). Wix's 38% is the best proxy and includes resellers.
- Wix Marketplace and GoDaddy Pro services economics not found.
- No Norwegian partner-program data (for example Norwegian Wix/Shopify partner counts).
